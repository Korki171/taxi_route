# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: the owner or dispatcher of a small taxi or private-hire (Mietwagen) company in Tirol. They regularly get transfer requests (mostly airport runs to ski resorts) that they cannot drive themselves and today pass them on to colleagues in WhatsApp groups.

Secondary: travel agencies and hotels that book transfers. They use the exchange for free.

## Product Purpose

Transferborsa is a B2B exchange where checked taxi and transfer companies hand off rides they cannot do and take over rides from colleagues. Success: a company posts a ride once, a checked colleague with a free vehicle takes it, and both sides have the same written record.

## Positioning

It replaces the WhatsApp group. Instead of half answers in a group chat with price and luggage agreed verbally, a ride is posted once with time, luggage and a fixed price (or open for offers). The first checked company with a free vehicle takes it at the fixed price, and for everyone else it is gone. Price, flight, luggage and cancellation terms end up in a PDF confirmation.

## Operating Context

- Rides are mostly airport transfers (Flughafen München, Innsbruck) to Tyrolean ski resorts (Kitzbühel, Mayrhofen, St. Anton, Sölden), often with ski equipment and minibuses.
- Companies are checked before they can take rides: concession, insurance and company data.
- Passenger names and numbers are only visible to the company that takes the ride.
- Pilot operation around Innsbruck; the team contacts new companies personally.
- The site is German first, with an English version.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fixed-price takeover (first come) or offers when the price is open.
- PDF confirmation for every booking.
- Flat monthly price per company, billed by invoice, no commission per ride (current plan from 39,99 € per month). Free for travel agencies and hotels.
- Salzburg, Zürich and South Tyrol are planned, not live.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Transferborsa.
- Accent colour on the live site is Zirbengrün (#2c5a4b); the owner rejected orange.
- Pictures must be real photographs (CC0 or own), not AI-generated imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

No customers, logos, quotes or figures may be shown yet. Proof on the page comes from the product itself (the ride card, the confirmation, the chat it replaces), never from invented testimonials or numbers.
