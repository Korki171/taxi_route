# Startseite (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: owner or dispatcher of a small taxi or private-hire company in Tirol. Action: register the company. Proof: the product itself (ride card, takeover, PDF confirmation, the chat it replaces); no customers or figures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The taxi roof sign owns the page: "Wer frei hat, übernimmt." A lit FREI sign is the one thing every taxi owner knows, and taking a ride switches it to BESETZT. Refuses the category default of a headline over a product screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Live-site world kept: cool paper ground, navy as the only ink, Zirbengrün only on actions and the open state, Imbue uppercase for sign-size words, Albert Sans for reading, double-rule section frames, real tables. New: one taxi-yellow (#f5c518) used only on a lit sign face with black lettering; an unlit sign is navy with grey lettering.

STORY: The visitor sees a lit FREI sign above a real ride card, presses "Zum Festpreis übernehmen", watches the sign go dark to BESETZT and the card read Vergeben, understands that the first free colleague takes the ride, then registers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline at display size, lead sentence, green register button and a link to the flow. Right, the yellow roof sign (about 380px wide) directly on top of the white ride card (route, time, load, 310 € fixed price, takeover button). Header with nav above.

FORM: Taxischild, dealt lead of the surface roll (structure 4 of 7 in my ranked list), chosen by the owner over the split-flap board. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
